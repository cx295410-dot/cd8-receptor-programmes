# External clinical outcome analysis plan

This post hoc exploratory extension uses GSE78220 for author-recorded response and OS, GSE91061 for response replication, and GSE135222 for PFS. Datasets were selected after the original single-cell study. The protocol below is fixed before statistical association testing in this extension.

Use pretreatment biopsies. Average log1p(FPKM) across repeated biopsies of a patient before gene standardisation and retain one clinical observation per patient. Verify duplicate-patient outcome concordance. CR/PR (or PRCR) defines response; SD/PD defines nonresponse. Other labels are excluded. Exclude missing survival time or unknown event status. Verify units, event coding, time origin and pretreatment status against primary sources before manuscript inclusion; mark provisional endpoints clearly when not verified.

Adapt the original six gene programmes to bulk expression: mean gene-wise within-cohort z-score of log1p of the supplied abundance (FPKM in the two melanoma cohorts; TPM in GSE135222), clipped to [-3,3], with at least half of markers measured. These are bulk-adapted scores and do not establish cell-intrinsic CD8 mechanisms. Memory and Dysfunction are the two primary programmes for this exploratory extension; analyse all six transparently. Use continuous univariable Cox HRs per score SD, fixed higher-score response AUC without reversing direction, two-sided Mann-Whitney tests and stratified bootstrap AUC CIs. Correct all 24 programme/endpoint tests with BH, and separately identify the eight tests covering the two primary programmes across four cohort-endpoint combinations in three cohorts. Median splits serve descriptive KM plots only, with risk tables and confidence intervals. Do not optimise survival cutoffs. Use PH diagnostics; no multivariable claims without adequate events and covariate data. Do not fit high-dimensional models, nomograms or DCA in these small cohorts.

Report patient selection, cohort-specific follow-up/event counts and missingness; do not pool OS with PFS. No untreated comparator exists, so treatment-specific prediction is not established. Retain all results regardless of significance. External cross-cancer evidence is an exploratory extension, not a direct replacement for pathology endpoints in the surgical cohort.

Documentation correction on 2026-10-27: the initial narrative mistakenly counted six Memory/Dysfunction tests from three cohorts. There are four cohort-endpoint combinations (Hugo response, Hugo OS, Riaz response, Kim/Jung PFS), giving eight tests. The implemented analysis used eight from its first run. This correction changes neither a hypothesis, a model, nor a numerical result. The explicit TPM wording records the source sample annotation verified before scoring.
